How to Read This Page
Table Columns
Dwell Time

How long from when the flight lands to when the driver picks up the passenger. This includes walking through the terminal, getting bags, and meeting the driver. Only applies to airport arrivals.

Drive Time

How long from picking up the passenger to dropping them off. The actual driving time on the road.

Gate → Drop (Full Trip)

The full trip time from when the flight arrives at the gate to when the passenger is dropped off. For arrivals this is dwell + drive combined. For returns/cruises it's the same as drive time.

The Numbers (Avg / Med / P75)
Avg (Average)

Add up all trip times and divide by how many trips. A few really long trips can make this number higher than what's "normal."

Med (Median)

The middle trip. Half your trips are faster, half are slower. Best answer for "how long does this usually take?"

P75 (75th Percentile)

75 out of 100 trips finish within this time. Good for planning with buffer. This is what the auto-scheduler uses.

Confidence

How much you can trust the numbers based on how many completed trips we have for that route:   High 20+ trips — very reliable   Medium 10-19 — decent estimate   Low 5-9 — rough estimate   None under 5 — not enough data yet
