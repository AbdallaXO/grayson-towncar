{% comment %} The Forecast half of the Service tab: what the fleet will need, soonest first, out to the chosen horizon. The one rule this template exists to hold: an ESTIMATE and a DEADLINE never look the same. A mileage interval is projected from how hard the car has been worked over the last 30 days, so it carries a "≈" and the rate it rests on. A date interval is a real calendar deadline and says so. Someone will book a shop day off this page; they have to be able to see which kind of number they are booking against. And what cannot be projected is COUNTED at the bottom, never dropped. An empty forecast that actually means "we do not know" is the one way this page could do real harm. {% endcomment %}
