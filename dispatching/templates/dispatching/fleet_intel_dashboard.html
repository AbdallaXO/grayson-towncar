How to read this (plain English):
  • Farmed out = we gave the job to an outside affiliate instead of one of our own drivers.
  • What we paid affiliates = total cash paid to outside companies for those jobs.
  • Could've kept in-house = what we paid the affiliate minus what it would've cost us to do it with our own driver (driver pay only — gas/tolls not counted yet). Positive = we likely lost money by farming; negative = the affiliate was actually cheaper, so farming was the right call.
  • % = that "could've kept" amount as a share of what we paid. 43% means for every $100 paid to the affiliate, about $43 could have stayed with us.
  • Data coverage = share of farmed jobs we have enough info to compare (we need the route's in-house rate). Lower coverage → trust the dollar totals a bit less.
  • These are opportunity numbers, not guaranteed savings — whether we could actually have done the job depends on the reason it was farmed. Use Find the leaks to see the proof per job.
