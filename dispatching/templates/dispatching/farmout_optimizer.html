Run this after you build the board, before you farm anything out. It never changes the schedule — it prices the farm-outs you're about to commit and flags the ones worth keeping.

For every job headed to an affiliate, it prices the trip against the whole roster ({{ roster_count }} carded affiliates) using your real DriverPayRate cards, then checks whether keeping it on one of your own drivers — and farming a cheaper leg to make room — costs less.

Feasibility uses the scheduled flight arrival, not hindsight, and fits each kept job inside the driver's actual working day. Far or unknown destinations are left uncomputed, so no recommendation rests on a guessed drive time.

Drive times calibrated to real Orlando routes:

MCO ↔ Disney · 30m    MCO ↔ Universal · 25m
MCO ↔ Sanford · 60m    Disney ↔ Port · 72m

Port Canaveral and Sanford are their own categories, judged purely on net-spend. Minimum saving to flag a swap: {{ min_savings_display }}.
