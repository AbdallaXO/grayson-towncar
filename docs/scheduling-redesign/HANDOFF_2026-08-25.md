## What the tool does now (after your two rulings today)

1. Plans at **your chosen crew** — never picks who works (the roster-size
   search was cut by the Ticket-C noise test; decision D15 ledger in 01 §1.1).
2. Searches **unit swaps** among the ticked crew (better coverage / cheaper
   farm-outs), tells you the change in plain words, you apply it yourself.
3. **"Catch the rest" (your D16 ruling today):** when trips are farming out
   while certified drivers sit available and cars sit free, it names them —
   *"Tick David, give him #13 Towncar — catches the 5:00 PM trip, ≈$71
   kept."* Checks his overnight rest, availability window, and Sprinter
   certification first; if nobody can reach the farmed trips it says so and
   why. Still propose-only — you tick, you call, you apply.
4. Prices every 13.5–15h long day as a choice; shows the engine's own
   rest-tight placements honestly (your D15 "accept for now" ruling).
