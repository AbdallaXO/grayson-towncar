# The schedule board now shows when two drivers are sharing a car

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — the schedule board now shows which drivers are sharing a car today.
>
> 1. The two drivers on one car sit together, boxed in a thin border. Their unit number turns navy with a two-people mark. Whoever has the car first is on top.
> 2. Under each name, a chip says when the car changes hands, like "Leo till ~2:35p".
> 3. A grey striped stretch marked "Car with Leo" shows when the other driver has it.
>
> Drag a job into that stretch and the row turns orange: you're asked before it's assigned, and you can still assign it anyway. A "Tight" tag means a close hand-over. "Clash" means a trip can't work as planned, and that trip gets a red marker. Point at it to see what to move.
>
> Nothing else changed. Drivers with their own car look the same.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** the schedule board (in-house view), in each shared driver's name column and timeline. Dragging onto a shared driver on the capacity planner asks the same question.

**Why:** on a day with 20+ drivers, a split car was only two matching unit numbers a few rows apart. It was easy to give a job to one driver while the other had the car. The warning for this existed but never popped up by itself, so dispatchers didn't see it.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "What does the striped stretch cover?" The other driver's trips, from the drive out to when the last one should be done. A job inside it needs the car back first.
- "Why 'Tight' when the day ran fine?" Tight means the hand-over is close. It often works; it's a heads-up, not an alarm. "Clash" means the car would be needed in two places, or would change hands twice.
- "Both drivers have a red marker." Either trip could move to fix it. Point at a marker and it says so.
- "It asked me before assigning — can I still do it?" Yes. Click Assign Anyway. Nothing is blocked. (The shared-car question follows the same on/off switch as the other assignment warnings.)
- "Does the driver app change?" No. Drivers already see who they share with.
