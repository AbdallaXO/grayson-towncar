# Undo a decision on the Commission check screen

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — if you click the wrong button on Commission check, you can now undo it.
>
> 1. Open Commission check and click the Decided tab.
> 2. Find the booking and click Undo.
>
> The booking goes back on its list to decide again. If it was marked "not commissionable", commission is switched back on.
>
> Commission that has already been paid can't be undone here. Nothing else on the screen changed.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** Affiliate Management → Commission check → Decided

**Why:** a mis-click shouldn't need a developer to fix.
