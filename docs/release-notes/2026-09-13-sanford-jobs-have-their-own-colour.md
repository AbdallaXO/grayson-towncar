# Sanford jobs have their own colour now

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — every Sanford job is now light blue on the board, so you can spot
> the other airport without reading a single address.
>
> 1. Open the schedule board. Any job going to or coming from Sanford is a pale
>    light blue bar — the only light bar on the page, so it jumps straight out.
> 2. Hover it and the popup says "Sanford (SFB)" right at the top.
> 3. Same colour on the day planner, and on the legs list you'll see a small
>    light blue SFB tag next to the trip type.
>
> Both directions are light blue, so all the Sanford work stacks at a glance.
>
> Nothing else moved. Orlando airport jobs are still blue arrivals and green
> returns, cruise is still orange, and watch-list jobs are still black bars.
> Nothing changed about how jobs are priced, assigned or paid.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** The schedule board, the dispatch board, the day planner, and the
legs list.

**Why:** Sanford is a different airport — about 40 minutes further north than
Orlando — but on the board it looked exactly like an MCO job, so it kept getting
assigned as if it were one, and the mistake only showed up once a car was already
committed.

**Expect to be asked:**
- *"Why is one bar pale and all the others dark?"* — that's the point. Every other
  bar on the board is a deep colour, so the one light bar is impossible to miss on
  a full day.
- *"What about a guest who lives in the city of Sanford?"* — not light blue. Only
  the airport itself counts, otherwise a big share of the board would be blue and
  the colour would stop meaning anything.
- *"Can I still tell if it's a pickup or a drop-off?"* — yes. The trip type is
  unchanged, and the hover popup spells out both ends of the job.
- *"A Sanford job is on my watch list and it's black, not blue."* — that's correct.
  Watch-list jobs stay black; the stripe down its left edge is light blue.
