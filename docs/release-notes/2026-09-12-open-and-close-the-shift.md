# Opening and closing the shift now have a proper checklist

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — opening and closing the shift now have a proper checklist.
>
> 1. Clock in as normal. If you're down to open, the list opens itself.
>    Otherwise it's under the Shift menu up top — you see the one you're down
>    for, and if you're not marked for either you see both.
> 2. Four rows count themselves off the board: unassigned trips, chauffeurs
>    who haven't confirmed, flight alerts nobody reviewed, and the turns the
>    new pickup times just broke. Each lists what's left and how long until
>    that pickup.
> 3. Five rows only you can answer: systems up, the "I'm opening" post, then
>    texts, email and GoHighLevel, one at a time. Click the line to tick it;
>    it records your name and the time.
> 4. Anything you can't clear gets a **Note**: what it is, who owns it, what
>    happens next.
> 5. Hit **Complete**, confirm, then copy the message it writes and send it here.
>
> Close Shift is the same, pointed at tomorrow. What you leave outstanding
> shows at the top of the next shift, with a name on it, until it's sorted.
>
> Nothing about the board changed — same trips, same assigning, same tasks,
> same driver app.

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** the Shift menu in the dispatcher top bar, next to Clock.
Clocking in sends the day's opener there automatically. Luis and Abdalla also get
"How shifts ran" in the same menu — the last seven days, one line a day.

**Why:** opening was a habit, not a standard. Nobody could say afterwards whether
the board was safe by 7:15, who checked the texts, or what was handed over. Now
it is written down, with a name against every loose end and a signature at the
end.

**Who counts as the opener:** whoever the staffing board says — the person with
the opener mark on that day, or the earliest person in if nobody is marked. It
reads the board rather than keeping its own answer, so the two can't disagree.

**The clock:** the two targets now follow the SOP's own measure — 45 minutes
from starting the open to a safe board, 90 to a finished open — with 7:15 and
8:00 kept underneath as a backstop. Whichever lands first is the one you're held
to, and the tile says which. Come in at 5 and you're due safe at 5:45; come in at
7 and it's 7:15, because a 7 AM pickup doesn't care what time you sat down.

**Counting rules worth knowing:** trips whose pickup has already gone by are
listed separately and don't hold the shift up. A chauffeur only counts as
confirmed when the chauffeur confirmed it — a dispatcher setting the status
doesn't clear that row. Each outstanding item carries a countdown to its pickup,
which turns red inside 45 minutes.

**Expect to be asked:**
- *"Why did it jump me to a checklist?"* — You're down to open. It only does that
  for the opener, and only while the opening is unfinished.
- *"I'm not the opener but I opened the list."* — Fine. Anyone can work it, and
  whoever completes it is the name recorded.
- *"Why can't I tick the top three?"* — They count themselves. A row you can tick
  is a row that stops meaning anything.
- *"Why aren't the tasks on the opening list?"* — About seventy a day, and two
  thirds come to nothing. Conflicts still show red on the board where you're
  already looking. The closing list does count tomorrow's, where the number is
  small.
- *"Where did 'missed calls' go?"* — Folded into the texts row. It was the same
  check twice.
- *"How does it know which jobs clash?"* — It already knows what each pickup
  was before you moved it. It runs the chauffeur's day as it stands, runs it
  again as it stood, and keeps the turns that got worse. Same arithmetic the
  board uses for its red badges, so the two can't disagree.
- *"Why doesn't it list every conflict?"* — Because step 4 asks what the new
  times broke, not what was already red. Standing conflicts stay on the board
  where you're already looking — about seventy a day, two thirds of them
  closing untouched.
- *"It's showing a clash I already sorted."* — Reload the page; the row clears
  once the turn actually works again. Acknowledging the time change on its own
  doesn't clear it, because that only means "I've seen it moved". Either way a
  move drops off by itself after twelve hours.
- *"Why can't I see Close Shift in the menu?"* — You're down as today's opener,
  so the menu shows you the open. The closer sees the close. If the staffing
  board has nobody marked for either, everyone sees both.
- *"It won't let me complete."* — Something is outstanding with nothing written
  against it. Leave a note and it lets you through. That's the point.
- *"Does it text the group by itself?"* — No. It writes the message, you send it.
- *"I completed it by mistake."* — Luis or Abdalla can reopen it.
