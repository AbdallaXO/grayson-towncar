# The shop-window finder stops sending you three weeks out

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — **When can I take a car down?** on the Fleet desk and the
> **Outlook** now give the same answer for the same car, and that answer is the
> soonest good day rather than the emptiest-looking one.
>
> 1. Once a window costs Dispatch nothing, the **sooner** one wins. It used to
>    pick a day nine days out over one tomorrow that cost exactly the same.
> 2. The number in each square is now how many cars are **free** that hour, not
>    how many are busy. Bigger is better. A minus number means more trips than
>    cars, so the extra ones already go to affiliates.
> 3. Saturday and Sunday rows now say **shop closed** on the Desk, the way the
>    Outlook already did.
>
> If a day is far enough out that its bookings are still coming in, it now says
> so — for example *"A Thursday usually runs 14 cars and 6 are booked so far."*
>
> Nothing about booking a window changed, and nothing here takes a car off the
> road on its own.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** Fleet → **Desk** → *When can I take a car down?*, and Fleet
→ **Outlook**. Both read one shared model, so they cannot disagree.

**Why:** ranking on "how much slack is left at the tightest hour" quietly
preferred the far end of the horizon, because demand is only what is **booked**
and a day three weeks out looks empty for the single reason that its bookings
have not arrived. The Desk capped at a week and the Outlook did not, so the same
car got two different best days — Wed 16 Sep on one screen and Thu 24 Sep on the
other, with the Outlook listing Wed 16 underneath as costing nothing.

**The rule now:** any window leaving two or more cars spare at its tightest hour
counts as equally comfortable, and among those the soonest wins. Days this car
tips into short still rank last, and still ask for a confirmation when booked.

**Expect to be asked:**
- *"The numbers in the grid all changed."* — Same data, opposite direction. They
  used to count cars out on trips; they now count cars still free.
- *"Why won't it offer me Saturday?"* — The yards are shut, and Saturday is our
  busiest day. That has not changed, it is just labelled now.
