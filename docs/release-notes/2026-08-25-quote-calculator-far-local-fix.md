# Quote Calculator prices farther custom trips correctly now

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — the Quote Calculator was underpricing some custom trips roughly 25 to 60 miles out (addresses like Winter Haven or Lakeland that aren't on our rate card). It was quoting them like a quick local run, which came out way too low. That's fixed.
>
> Nothing to click differently — quote it the same way you always have. Trips inside about 25 miles of one of our usual pickup areas still price the same as before.
>
> If you quoted one of these farther custom trips recently and the number seemed low, it was — pull a fresh quote before you confirm anything with the guest.
>
> Local runs, airport transfers, and everything already on the rate card are unaffected.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** the Quote Calculator page (dispatcher nav)

**Why:** the tool decided "local, no empty-return charge" vs. "out-of-town, full charge" using the wrong distance cutoff, so trips up to 60 miles out were getting the local discount meant for a few-mile hop near MCO or Disney.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "Was I quoting these wrong?" — No, the tool was giving you a bad number. Not your error.
- "How do I know which trips this affected?" — Anything off the rate card, 25–60 miles out, quoted in the last few weeks. If in doubt, requote it.
