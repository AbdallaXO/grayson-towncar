# Texting the guest now updates the trip status too

## Send this to the team

> Drivers — one tap now does both. When you send the guest your "On the way"
> text, the trip is marked On the way at the same moment. Same for On location,
> and the review text marks the guest as picked up. You'll see a quick
> confirmation, and the card refreshes when you come back from Messages.
>
> It only ever moves the status FORWARD — texting never knocks a trip back a
> step, and it never marks a trip completed. Completing stays its own button,
> with its own "are you sure".
>
> Dispatch — this is why a driver can show On the way without touching the
> status buttons: he texted the guest, and the board now believes him. The
> status buttons still work exactly as before for drivers who prefer them.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** the "Text the guest" chips on the trip card in the driver
app; the effect shows on every dispatch board that reads trip status.

**Why:** the chips carry the same words as the status steps, and on day one a
driver texted "on the way" while his trip still read as not started — he
reasonably assumed the tap told dispatch where he was. The rate math on the
Guest Communication page already treated a text as proof the moment had
arrived; now the status record agrees instead of contradicting it.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "Does the text move a trip backwards?" — never. Forward only, and only up to
  the step the text belongs to.
- "Can a driver still set status without texting?" — yes, the status buttons
  and dropdown are unchanged.
