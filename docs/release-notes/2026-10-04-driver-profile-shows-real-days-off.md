# A driver's profile now shows their real days off and time off

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — the Weekly Schedule box on a driver's profile page now shows their days off and time off correctly.
>
> - A day they don't work says OFF.
> - Each upcoming time-off entry says what it is — Off (full day), Until 4 PM, Window 8 AM – 2:30 PM — the same wording as the schedule board.
> - Time off that already started, like a vacation that began Monday, keeps showing until it ends, with both dates.
> - A request the driver sent that hasn't been approved yet has a yellow PENDING tag. Denied or cancelled requests don't show at all.
>
> Before, days off read as Full Day and every entry just said "Override".
>
> Nothing changed on the schedule board, and nothing about how you enter or approve time off.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** a driver's profile page (Drivers → All Drivers → click a driver), the
Weekly Schedule box in the left column.

**Why:** the box was reading the wrong details, so a driver's day off looked like a working
day, every time-off entry said "Override", denied requests showed as if they were live, and
a vacation that started before today vanished from the list. The defaults line under an
empty schedule also read "Full_Day, 6:00 – 23:00"; it now reads "Full Day, 6 AM – 11 PM".

**Expect to be asked:**
- "Does PENDING mean the driver is off?" No. A pending request changes nothing on the
  schedule board or in auto-assign until someone approves it — the tag is there so you
  know one is waiting.
- "Why can't I see time off from six weeks out?" The box only looks 30 days ahead, same as
  before.
