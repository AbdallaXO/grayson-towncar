# The team lead can approve refunds and see the whole team's day

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — we now have a dispatch team lead on the floor. Refund requests can be approved by the lead as well as by Abdalla, so they get handled on the lead's shift instead of waiting.
>
> 1. Request a refund from the trip exactly as before, and pick the right type: Price Adjustment, Partial Cancellation or Full Cancellation.
> 2. The lead sees it waiting and approves it or turns it down.
>
> The lead also has a Team page showing who's on today, what's on each person's plate and what's been done so far, so they know where to lend a hand.
>
> Nothing changed in how you ask for a refund or in the three types, and your own clock and My Schedule work the same. If a refund goes through as the wrong type, tell Abdalla — that fix is still Abdalla's.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** Making someone lead is one box in the admin: Users → open the person → Groups → add "Dispatch Lead" → Save. Take it away the same way, no deploy. The lead then gets **Team** and **Refunds** (with a count of requests waiting) in the top bar, and Who's on now and Staffing board at the bottom of the Shift menu. On the open and close checklists, each open note gets a Hand off button.

**The Team page:** the refunds waiting, the tasks nobody has picked up, and whether today's open and close checklists are done — then one simple line per dispatcher on today: right now (Busy, Idle, On a break, Not in, Done for the day), how many reservations they booked today, how many tasks they finished, and the last thing they did ("Assigned a driver · 2:41 PM"). Tap a line for where they are against their hours and what's on their plate. The team's totals sit at the bottom; everyone else is listed as off.

**Why:** Refunds waited for Abdalla, and the only way to let anyone else approve one was full admin — which also opens revenue, profit, payroll, pay rates, commissions and the admin site. The lead gets the refunds and the team view and none of that.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "Can the lead see what I'm doing?" Yes, on the Team page: whether you're clocked in against your hours, what's assigned to you, how many reservations you've booked and tasks you've finished today, and the last thing you did in the app. Never pay or revenue.
- "What does Idle mean?" Clocked in, with nothing done in the app for 30 minutes or more. The page says plainly that they may be on the phone — it's a nudge to check in, not a timesheet.
- "Can the lead see revenue, pay, or the hours report?" No. Analytics, KPIs, payroll, driver pay, pay rates, the hours report and its export stay with Abdalla. Who's on now shows who is clocked in or on a break right now, since when, and how long they've worked so far today — no totals over days, no history, no export.
- "Can the lead change my schedule or approve my time off?" No. The staffing board is look only for the lead, and time-off requests, with their notes, still go to Abdalla.
- "Can the lead approve their own refund?" Yes, by Abdalla's choice. Every approval shows who made it on the Refunds page.
- "Can the lead fix a refund that went through as the wrong type?" No. The Correct button stays Abdalla's.
- "What if Abdalla and the lead both open the same request?" The first decision wins, even if the other click lands while the refund is still going through. The second person is told it was already done, or is going through, and asked to refresh; nothing they pressed takes effect.
