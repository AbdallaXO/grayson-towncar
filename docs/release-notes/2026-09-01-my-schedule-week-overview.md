# My Schedule now opens with a real week at a glance

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — your My Schedule page got a refresh.
>
> It now opens with your actual week: seven cards with real dates showing your
> hours each day, whether you're in the office or working from home, who you're
> on with, and any time off — all changes already included, so what you see is
> what's really happening. Today is highlighted, and there's a "Next week"
> button to see the week ahead.
>
> Tap any day card and the detail below switches to that day — the timeline of
> who's on, the handoffs, and anyone out.
>
> Nothing else changed: requesting time off works the same at the bottom of the
> page, and the on-call banner still shows who's covering tonight.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** My Schedule (the dispatcher's own page in the top nav).

**Why:** the old page led with a dateless "standard week" that ignored one-off
changes, so a dispatcher couldn't tell at a glance what this actual week — or
next week — looked like for them.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "Next week shows me off but I usually work that day" — the cards show real
  dates with approved time off and one-off changes applied, so they can differ
  from the usual pattern. That's the point.
- The days-on / hours count at the top is the viewer's scheduled time for that
  week, split shifts included.
