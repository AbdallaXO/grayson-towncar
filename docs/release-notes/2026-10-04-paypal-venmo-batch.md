# Pay every PayPal and Venmo agent with one upload

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — you can now pay every travel agent and agency on PayPal or Venmo with one upload, instead of one at a time.
>
> 1. On Affiliate Management, click **PayPal & Venmo batch**. Pick **Everyone**, **Agents**, or **Agencies** at the top.
> 2. Click **Download file**, then upload it in PayPal under Payouts and send it.
> 3. Come back and click **Mark these paid**. Only the group you picked gets marked.
>
> If we can't read someone's PayPal email or Venmo handle, they're listed at the bottom with a Fix link and left out of the file. If someone's amount changes between downloading and marking paid, they stay unpaid in the app and the screen tells you what to do.
>
> Tap any agent or agency name, here or on Affiliate Management, to open their profile.
>
> Nothing changed for Zelle, Cash App, check, or bank agents. Pay them the same way as before.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** Affiliate Management → **PayPal & Venmo batch** button, top right.

**Why:** Paying about 80 PayPal/Venmo payees one at a time took hours. PayPal's Payouts upload pays PayPal and Venmo from a single file.

**Expect to be asked:**
- *"Why isn't my agent under Agents?"* — Agents means the ones we pay directly, same as the Direct Agents tab. If their agency collects their pay, they're inside that agency's amount.
- *"Do agents get their statement email?"* — Not from the batch. Send one from Payout History if someone asks.
- *"PayPal rejected a row."* — PayPal shows failed rows before you send. Fix that agent's handle on their profile and pay them on their own.
- *"Can I upload the file twice?"* — No. PayPal would pay everyone twice. Marking paid twice in the app is harmless, because the second click finds nobody owed.
- *"Why is someone missing?"* — Either nothing is ready to pay them yet (for example, the trip finished less than a day ago) or their handle is in the "Left out" list.
