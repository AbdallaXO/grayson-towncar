# Driver Conflict tasks only turn red when it's actually urgent

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — Driver Conflict tasks on the board now only turn red when there's a real problem.
>
> Before, a driver running just a couple minutes behind got the same red "Critical" badge as one who genuinely wasn't going to make the next pickup — so the red badge had stopped meaning much.
>
> Now: a driver who's a little tight between two jobs but still going to make it shows up as a lower-key "Tight turn" card — a heads-up to keep an eye on it, nothing to act on yet. A driver who genuinely won't make the next pickup still shows Critical, and that pickup also gets an eye-flag on the board so it stays visible even if you're not on the Tasks screen.
>
> Nothing changed about how you resolve one — same Call, Text, and Reassign buttons as before. And nothing texts or calls a driver automatically — that's still on you.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** Ops Control → Tasks (Driver Conflict and the new Tight Turn cards), plus the eye-flag icon on the schedule board and legs dashboard.

**Why:** The red / Overdue / Escalated badges fired on almost any conflict, however small, regardless of how much real slack the driver had — so dispatchers had learned to discount them. The badge is now tied to whether the driver can actually make the pickup, not just whether he's a minute behind schedule.

**Expect to be asked:**
- *"Did the resolution steps change?"* — No, same Call / Text / Reassign buttons, same order.
- *"What's a 'Tight turn' task?"* — A driver's turnaround between two jobs is close but should still work out. It's a watch item, not an emergency — it clears itself if the gap opens back up, or escalates to a real Driver Conflict if it doesn't.
- *"Does this text or call the driver automatically?"* — No. Nothing automated touches drivers; a human still makes that call.
- *"Why does a pickup suddenly show an eye icon?"* — The system flagged it because the driver genuinely won't make it on schedule — same watch-flag you'd raise by hand, just raised automatically for a real conflict.
