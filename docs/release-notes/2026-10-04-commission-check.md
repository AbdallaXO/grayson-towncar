# Check personal trips and duplicate bookings before paying agents

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — there's a new Commission check screen to catch commissions we shouldn't pay.
>
> 1. Go to Affiliate Management and click Commission check.
> 2. **Possible personal trips:** the rider looks like the agent, the notes mention a discount, or the price is well under the normal rate. Click "Not commissionable" or "It's fine".
> 3. **Possible duplicates:** same agent, same rider, same day, booked twice. Click "Don't pay this one" on the copy, or "All real, pay each".
>
> Nothing changes until you click, and once you decide, it doesn't come back.
>
> The bookings themselves aren't touched. This only decides whether the agent gets commission.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** Affiliate Management → Commission check

**Why:** about 113 possible personal trips ($2,337) and 21 possible duplicate pairs were sitting in the unpaid commissions.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "The agent's own email is on a client's booking. Why isn't it flagged?" Agents do that all the time, so it isn't a sign of a personal trip.
- "I marked one by mistake." Tell Abdalla. It can be switched back, but there is no undo button on this screen yet.
