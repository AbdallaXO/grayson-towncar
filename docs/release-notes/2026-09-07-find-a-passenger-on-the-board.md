# Look a passenger up from the schedule board

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — there's a search box on the schedule board now. When a guest calls
> and you don't know what day they're on, you don't have to leave the board to
> find out.
>
> 1. Click **Find a passenger** at the top of the board (or just press the `/`
>    key) and type their name, their phone number, their email, or the trip
>    number off their confirmation.
> 2. Their trips come up underneath — soonest first, with the time, the route and
>    the chauffeur on each one.
> 3. Click the trip. The board jumps to that day and rings the trip in gold.
>
> Type the phone number however it's written in front of you — with or without
> the dashes and brackets, it'll find them either way.
>
> If a trip is with an affiliate, the board switches over to the affiliate side
> on its own. If it was cancelled, it says so instead of sending you looking for
> a bar that isn't there.
>
> Nothing else on the board changed. Same drivers, same lanes, same drag and
> drop.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** the top of the schedule board, between the In-House /
Affiliates switch and the driver filter.

**Why:** the board is keyed on one day, and a guest on the phone never leads with
the date. Finding them meant opening the reservations list in another tab,
reading the date off a row, then coming back and paging the board to it.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "It found two of the same person." Duplicate guest records already exist in the
  system; the search shows both so you can see which one holds the trip.
- "Where's their trip from last year?" The panel carries the last six months plus
  everything ahead. It tells you how many trips are on file in total — the rest
  are on the reservation.
- "I clicked a trip and the board says it's on a different day." That trip moved
  after the panel loaded. The line at the top has a link straight to it.
- "Does this reach the chauffeurs?" No. Nothing about the driver app changed.
