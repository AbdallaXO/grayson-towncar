# Day Setup now plans split shifts and grades car handoffs

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — Suggest Day Setup now helps with second shifts and shared cars.
>
> 1. Build the day like normal. Open Suggest Day Setup again and you'll see a
>    "Second shifts" list: who from the bench could take a car for the evening,
>    which trips they'd catch, and roughly what it saves. Tick one and hit
>    Apply if you want it on the plan — nobody gets called automatically, you
>    still make that call.
> 2. Any car with two drivers now shows a green, yellow, or red tag. Green
>    means the wash-fuel-and-back timing works. Yellow means it only works
>    with a plan — wash the night before or hand it over at the airport. Red
>    means don't plan it.
> 3. Each driver's row shows their planned day length, and it flags anything
>    past 13.5 hours.
>
> When you apply a shared car, both drivers' morning/evening windows are saved
> so the car can't get double-booked.
>
> Nothing else changed — same buttons, same Apply, nothing happens on its own.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** the Suggest Day Setup window on the capacity planner, same
button as before. The handoff tags also ride on the second-shift suggestions.

**Why:** second shifts on shared cars are the one lever that measurably beats
farming trips out (about $78k/yr at current volume), and until now every split
was arranged by hand with nothing written down. The timing grades come from
the real handoff routine — drop the guest, El Car Wash, fuel, back to the
warehouse — measured against every handoff we've actually run.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "It suggested calling someone in for one trip — is that a mistake?" No — it
  flags those as "thin — worth it?" with the dollars. There's no call-out
  minimum, so it's your judgement: offer, let the driver decline.
- "Why does a yellow tag mention the flight time?" If the second driver's
  first job is an airport arrival, the plan has to survive the flight moving.
  Leave the slack it asks for.
- "Where do second-shift suggestions come from on an empty day?" They read the
  built board, so they only appear once the day has jobs assigned.
