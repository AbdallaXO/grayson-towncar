# The Guest Communication page updates the moment a driver taps a status

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — the Guest Communication page now moves in real time. When a chauffeur
> hits On the way and sends his text, the page shows it that same minute — no more
> waiting for the whole trip to be finished before anything appears.
>
> Two more things on that page:
> 1. There's a **Today** button next to 7 days and 30 days, so you can watch just
>    today's trips as they happen.
> 2. You can pick any two dates and hit **Go** to see exactly that stretch.
>
> A chauffeur who hasn't used the texting buttons yet shows as "Not started"
> instead of a red zero — fair, since this is all brand new.
>
> Nothing changed for the drivers: same buttons, same texts, same taps. Only the
> office page reads differently.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** the Guest Communication page (admin only), and the Guest
Communication panel on each driver's profile.

**Why:** on launch day a driver hit On the way, his text was logged, and the page
still showed nothing — the old math only counted trips after they were marked
completed. Now each text is scored at its own moment: on-the-way counts once the
driver is on the way, on-location once he's at the pickup, review once the guest
is aboard.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "The Texts logged counter moved but the rate didn't" — the tap was on a trip
  outside the date range being viewed. Switch to Today or widen the range.
- "Why does someone show Not started instead of a percentage?" — they've had
  chances but haven't used the buttons yet. The percentages start the first time
  they do.
