# Link agents to their agency in one click

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — a lot of agents chose "pay my agency" when they signed up, but were never linked to that agency, so nobody could pay them. There's now a screen to fix that in a few clicks.
>
> 1. On Affiliate Management, click **Agency links**.
> 2. The ones under **Ready to link** are already ticked. Tick any under **Check first** that are the same agency, then click **Link**.
> 3. If their agency isn't in the system yet, click **Add agency & link**, then add how that agency gets paid on its profile.
>
> Once linked, their commission goes out with the agency's payout.
>
> Agents who picked Venmo, PayPal, or anything else are still paid directly, even if they belong to an agency. They never show on this screen.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** Affiliate Management → **Agency links** button, top right.

**Why:** The Oct 4 payout scan found 78 agents owed money who chose "Agency" but weren't paid through one, so they sat under Direct Agents with no way to pay them.

**Expect to be asked:**
- *"Why is a linked agent still not getting paid?"* — Their agency has no payment method yet. The screen flags those with a warning. Add the method on the agency's profile.
- *"I linked the wrong one."* — Open the agent's profile and change the agency or turn "agency pays" off. Every link made here is recorded with who did it.
- *"Someone's in Check first but it's clearly the same agency."* — The name is spelled a little differently (like "LLC" on the end), so it waits for a person to confirm. Tick it and link.
