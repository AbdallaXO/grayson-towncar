# Agency links are grouped by agency

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — on the Agency links screen, agents are now grouped under their agency, so you can deal with a whole agency at once.
>
> 1. Each agency shows as one line with how many agents it has and what's owed. Click the name to see the agents.
> 2. The box next to the agency ticks or unticks all of its agents.
> 3. If the agency isn't in the system yet, open it and click "Add … & link" to add it once and link everyone you ticked.
>
> Searching still works and opens the agencies it finds.
>
> Nothing about who gets linked changed: only agents who chose "my agency pays me" ever show here.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** Affiliate Payments → Agency links

**Why:** Best Day Ever alone had about 50 agents in one long list. Grouping makes it one line to check.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "Two spellings of the same agency?" They're grouped together, and the agency is saved the way most of its agents typed it. You can rename it on the agency's profile.
