# The Fleet screens all look like one product now

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — small one. **Vehicles**, **Report** and a single car's page now
> look like the rest of Fleet: same headings, same round tab buttons across the
> top. They were the three screens still on the older look.
>
> The inspection form has those tabs too — you can get to any other Fleet screen
> from it now instead of going back first.
>
> And taking a car off the road is called **Take off the road** everywhere. The
> desk said one thing, the confirmation another and the car's own page a third.
>
> Nothing moved and nothing behaves differently — same tables, same numbers,
> same buttons in the same places.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** Fleet → **Vehicles**, **Report**, and any single car's page.
Plus the inspection form.

**Why:** four of the seven fleet screens had been redesigned and three had not,
so the top bar walked you between two visibly different products. The inspection
form carried no tabs at all, which made it the only fleet page you could not
navigate away from except by going back.

**Scope:** headers and the tab row only. The tables, cards and figures on those
pages are untouched.

**The name:** *Take off road* on the desk, *Take it off the road* in its
confirmation, *Take out of service* on the car page — one action, three names,
and the Inspections footnote pointed at the name that wasn't on the page it sent
you to. It is **Take off the road** in all of them now. The state a car is in is
still called *out of service* where Dispatch already reads it that way.

**Expect to be asked:**
- *"Did the Vehicles table change?"* — No. Only the heading above it.
