# Red conflict flags clear themselves the moment you fix the problem

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — the red conflict flags on the board are worth reading again. Fix the
> problem and the flag clears itself.
>
> Nothing to click. Hand the tight trip to a different chauffeur and the red is
> gone the next time the board draws — you'll watch it clear as you work.
>
> What's different: flags used to go up and never come down. This morning the
> board showed nine and exactly one was real; the other eight were left over from
> problems already sorted hours earlier. That's why the red stopped meaning
> anything. Those are gone.
>
> What did NOT change: any flag you raised is yours, and so is any flag you've
> touched — once you set it to Backup Arranged or write a note on it, it's yours
> and nothing clears it but you. A real conflict still shows red and still needs a
> chauffeur sorted. Nothing about how conflicts are spotted changed, and nothing
> messages a chauffeur.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** the schedule board — the red conflict flags on a trip

**Why:** the system raised a watch flag whenever a chauffeur was projected to miss
a pickup, but nothing ever took one down. The flag outlived the conflict, so the
board filled with red that was no longer true and dispatchers learned to ignore
all of it. Today's board: nine flags, one real.

**Expect to be asked:**
- *"Did it delete my flag?"* — No. Only flags the system raised **and** nobody has
  touched. The moment a dispatcher edits one, it belongs to them.
- *"How fast?"* — Reassigning a trip clears it immediately. Everything else
  clears on the next board check, within half an hour.
- *"I reset the whole day and the flags are still up."* — Expected, for up to half
  an hour. A full reset doesn't go through the per-trip path, so those clear on the
  next check rather than instantly.
- *"Why is that one still red?"* — Because it's real. Three were showing when this
  went out and all three were genuine.
