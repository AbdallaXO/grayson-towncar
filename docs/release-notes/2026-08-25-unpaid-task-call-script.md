# Words to use on an unpaid call, and the reservation one click away

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — two things on the tasks list.
>
> 1. **Open the reservation from any task.** Click the blue R-number on a task and the reservation opens in a new tab, so you keep your place in the list.
> 2. **Words to use on an unpaid call.** Open an unpaid task and you'll see a **What to say** button next to the Call button. It gives you the opening, already filled in with your name, the guest's name, their date and time, and how soon the trip is.
>
> It's written as a confirmation call, not a chase — you're calling to confirm their trip, the card is just how it gets confirmed. It stops after you ask for the card, so you pause there and let them talk.
>
> It's a starting point, not something you have to stick to — say it in your own words.
>
> Nothing changed about how you log a call, take a payment, or cancel a trip. Same buttons, same order.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** Ops Control → Tasks — the R-number on every task row, and the **What to say** button on the Call step of an unpaid task.

**Why:** Newer dispatchers were hesitating on these calls, and the money question is the hard one to open cold. Framing it as a confirmation call gets a much warmer reception than leading with the balance — the guest hears someone checking their trip is still on. The text version of this already existed for the Text step; this gives the call the same treatment. The reservation link also fixes the "Cancel trip" button on that page, which pointed at a dead address.

**Expect to be asked:**
- *"Is this sent to the guest automatically?"* — No. Nothing sends. It's words on a screen for you to say on the phone.
- *"It says [your name] instead of my name."* — That account has no first name saved on it. Add one and the script picks it up.
- *"Why doesn't it say the amount?"* — On purpose. The balance is already at the top of the page for you; leading with a dollar figure makes it a collections call. Quote it once they're ready to pay.
- *"Do other task types get one?"* — Only unpaid balances for now.
- *"Does the email confirmation really go out?"* — Yes, once the card goes through.
