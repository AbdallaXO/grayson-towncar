# The day only marks a gap as usable if the car can really get to base and back

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — on Fleet → The day, the gold marks between trips are now honest about driving time.
>
> A gap is only marked if the car can drive from where it drops to base, get its 20-minute walk-around, and still drive from base to its next pickup on time. Base to MCO is about 12 minutes, base to Disney about 35.
>
> The label now says how long the car actually sits at base, like "30m at base", instead of the whole gap. Hover over it to see the drive in and the drive out.
>
> So a 54-minute gap before a Disney pickup no longer shows up. Nothing changes on the schedule board, and no trips or drivers are moved.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** Fleet → The day (the gold marks), and the car suggested on Inspections.

**Why:** The page used to allow a flat 30 minutes to get to base and back, wherever the car was. That marked "54m free" before a Disney pickup that really needs 67 minutes (12 in from MCO, 20 walking, 35 out to Disney).

**Expect to be asked:**
- "Where do the 12 and 35 come from?" Base is 6785 Narcoossee Rd. MCO about 12, and Disney, Universal, I-Drive and the other hotels about 35, are the founder's figures; homes 30, Sanford 40 and the port 45 are estimates and easy to change.
- "Fewer gaps show up now." Yes, on purpose — the ones that dropped out were ones a driver couldn't actually make.
