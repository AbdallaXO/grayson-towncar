# Conflict flags come down on their own once the conflict is gone

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — a red conflict flag on the board now disappears by itself the moment
> the conflict is actually sorted. You can trust the red again.
>
> Nothing to click. Give a tight trip to a different chauffeur, or let the flight
> settle, and the flag clears within half an hour on its own.
>
> What's different: flags used to go up and never come down. This morning the board
> showed nine and only one was real — the other eight were leftovers from problems
> already fixed hours earlier. Those are cleared.
>
> What did NOT change: the flags you raise yourself stay exactly where you put them.
> Nothing removes those but you. And a genuine conflict still shows red and still
> needs a chauffeur sorted — one is on the board right now.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** the schedule board — the red conflict flags on a trip

**Why:** the system raised a watch flag whenever a chauffeur was projected to miss a
pickup, but nothing ever took it down. The flag outlived the conflict, so the board
filled with red that was no longer true and dispatchers started ignoring all of it.
Today's board: nine flags, one real.

**Expect to be asked:**
- *"Did it delete my flag?"* — No. Only flags the system raised on its own are
  cleared automatically. Anything a person raised is untouched, always.
- *"How fast does it clear?"* — Next board check, so within half an hour. It's not
  instant on the click.
- *"Why is there still one red flag?"* — Because that one is real: the chauffeur
  genuinely doesn't make that pickup and needs coverage.
