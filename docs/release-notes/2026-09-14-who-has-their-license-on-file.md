## Send this to the team

> Hey team — the driver directory (Drivers → All Drivers) now shows every chauffeur's paperwork: driver's license, chauffeur permit and DOT card, each marked on file, expiring, expired, photo sent but details not entered, or not on file.
>
> 1. Open Drivers → All Drivers.
> 2. The strip at the top counts who is missing a license, who is missing a permit, what's expired or about to be, and what needs a second look. Click a number to see just those drivers.
> 3. On a driver who still owes a document, tap "Text … to ask". It opens your Messages app with the request already written. Nothing goes out until you hit send.
>
> Search now also finds a driver by phone number or email.
>
> Nothing changed about how drivers send their documents — still Documents in the driver app — and nothing changed on the driver profile, pay, or the board.

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** the driver directory — Drivers → All Drivers in the dispatch menu.

**Why:** seven of forty active drivers have a license on file and there was no way to see which ones without opening forty profiles. The old page only flagged a document once it was expired or about to expire; a driver who never sent one looked identical to a driver who did.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "It says photo sent, details not entered — what do I do?" The driver did their part. Someone in the office opens their profile, reads the number and expiry off the photo, and types them in. Until then it counts under "Needs a look", not "Missing".
- "The DOT card says not on file for everyone." It's optional and not counted anywhere. It only matters for drivers who need one.
- "Does the text go out automatically?" No. The link opens your own Messages app with the words pre-written; you read it and hit send, or don't.
- "Why does the missing count not match the list?" The counts are for active chauffeurs only. Untick "Active only" and the twenty inactive drivers show too; operators (partners who send their own drivers) are never counted.
