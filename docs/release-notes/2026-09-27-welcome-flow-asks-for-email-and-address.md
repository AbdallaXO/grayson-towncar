# A new driver's welcome flow now asks for their last name, email, and home address before they're done

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — small change to the new-driver welcome link. When a driver sets up their login and lands on My Details, it now makes them fill in their last name, email, and home address before it saves, alongside the mobile number and license photo it already asked for. Then it sends them on to photograph their permit, same as before.
>
> The idea is to collect everything while they've got the phone in hand and are expecting to fill things in, instead of chasing them for an email or an address weeks later.
>
> Nothing changed for drivers who already have a login. My Details still lets them fix a phone number or address without filling in anything else, and email is still optional there.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** Driver app → My Details, only when reached from a welcome link.

**Why:** The Onboarding checklist on a driver's profile kept showing "Email: missing" and "Home address: missing" for weeks after a driver started, because the welcome flow let them skip both.

**Expect to be asked:**
- *"A driver has no email."* — They need one to finish onboarding now. Any address works; it's where pay statements go.
- *"Can I skip it for someone?"* — Not from the welcome link. Fill the fields in yourself on their profile and they won't be asked.
