# The booking screen reads the trip back to you before you book it

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — the booking screen is rebuilt, and the last step now writes the whole
> trip out as one sentence. **Read it back to the guest word for word, tick the
> box, then book.** That is what catches a wrong date, time, car or price before
> it is saved.
>
> On the trip step:
>
> 1. Under the pickup time the date is spelled out in full — "Saturday, September
>    5, 2026 at 10:30 AM · in 4 days". Check that line, not the boxes.
> 2. AM and PM are two separate buttons now, and neither starts picked. Choose one.
> 3. Put the **date and the flight in before the time**. It looks the flight up and
>    suggests a pickup — the landing time on an arrival, and two and a half hours
>    before takeoff on a resort run, so the guest is inside two hours early. Coming
>    off a ship it suggests nothing: that pickup is timed off when they disembark,
>    not off the flight.
>
> The screen never sets a time by itself. Press **Use suggested time** to take it,
> and type over it whenever the trip needs something else.
>
> Cars are picked from photos now — check the guest and their bags actually fit.
> And the suggested price already includes the $20 after-hours fee for a 10 PM to
> 6 AM pickup.
>
> Nothing else about pricing changed. You can still charge whatever you want.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** the dispatcher booking screen, all five steps.

**Why:** almost every booking mistake we chase later is one of five things — wrong
AM/PM, wrong date, wrong price, a car too small for the party, or a return leg
booked before the outbound. The screen now catches all five while the dispatcher
is still on the phone, and the read-back makes a wrong date impossible to miss
because you have to say it out loud.

The after-hours fee was the quiet one: the guest booking form has always added
$20 for a 10 PM–6 AM pickup, but a dispatcher had to know the rule and type it in,
so it went uncollected. The suggested rate now carries it, named, and the fee is
only marked as collected when the price actually covers it.

**Expect to be asked:**
- *"Why won't it let me book?"* — Something in red has to be fixed first, and the
  line next to the button says which. Orange items are only worth confirming with
  the guest; tick the box and carry on.
- *"Where did the custom gratuity box go?"* — Gratuity is now 15 / 18 / 20 / 25%
  buttons off the base price. Anything unusual goes in Additional Charges.
- *"I have to tick a box now?"* — Yes, one: that you read the trip back. That is
  the whole point of the last step.
- *"It says AM/PM is missing."* — Neither is picked by default any more. Guessing
  wrong moved a pickup twelve hours, so now it asks.
