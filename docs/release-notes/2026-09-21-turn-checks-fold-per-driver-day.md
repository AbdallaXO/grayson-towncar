# Tight-turn and conflict tasks fold into one row per driver per day, and tomorrow's sit under today's

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — Ops Control is a lot shorter today. Tight turn and driver
> conflict tasks now come as one row per driver per day, and the ones for
> tomorrow and later are folded up under today's work.
>
> 1. A row like "George Hill — 4 turns to check tomorrow" is the whole group.
>    Click it (or Show 4) to open the individual tasks underneath.
> 2. Claim all or Complete all does the whole group in one click. Each task
>    still has its own buttons once the group is open.
> 3. At the bottom of the list, "Tomorrow and later" holds every turn check
>    that isn't today's problem yet. Click Show to open it; it stays open on
>    that computer until you hide it.
>
> What did not change: the tasks themselves. Every one is still there, still
> counted in the lane totals, and the Future Blockers lane still lists them
> all. Nothing on the schedule board changed, and the red conflict flags on
> trips work exactly as before. A driver with just one turn check shows the
> same single row as always.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** Ops Control (the task queue), in the Unclaimed, Mine and
Others lanes. The Future Blockers lane shows the same groups but keeps them in
its main list, since that lane is the future view.

**Why:** the scanner files one tight-turn or conflict task per pair of legs.
Measured in August at about 71 a day, a third of the day's trips, with two
thirds of them closing without anyone moving anything. On a busy next-day board
that buried the payment chases and flight checks a dispatcher actually came in
to do. This is a display change only: tasks are grouped when they render, by
driver and pickup date. The scanner, the auto-closer, the board flags and the
recovery advisor all still work on the individual tasks, untouched.

**Expect to be asked:**
- *"Where did tomorrow's conflicts go?"* Bottom of the lane, under "Tomorrow
  and later." Click Show. They are also in the Future Blockers lane.
- *"Complete all — does that fix the turn?"* No. It clears the tasks, same as
  completing each one by hand would. Moving a job or reassigning the driver is
  still done on the board. The confirmation says so before it runs.
- *"Why is a group red when only one turn is a real conflict?"* The group takes
  the most urgent task's colour and sits in that priority section. The row's
  second line says how many the driver won't make versus how many are just
  tight.
- *"Why didn't the count on the lane tab go down?"* It counts tasks, not rows,
  so it still matches what the scanner filed. Only the number of rows dropped.
