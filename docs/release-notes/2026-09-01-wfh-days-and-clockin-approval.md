# The schedule now shows who's in the office, and off-schedule clock-ins wait for approval

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — two changes on the schedule and the clock.
>
> First, your schedule now says where you're working. Days marked WFH show a
> little house; office days show a building. You'll see it on your clock page
> and on the schedule board. If a usual work-from-home day is flipped to
> in-office for one date (say, for a meeting), it's marked on that date only —
> the rest of your week stays as it was.
>
> Second, clocking in outside your scheduled hours now needs a quick approval
> first. Hitting Clock In on a day off or outside your window sends Abdalla a
> request instead of starting the clock — the page says "waiting for approval"
> and checks for the answer every minute. Once it's approved, the page shows
> "you can clock in now", and the clock starts when you press Clock In. Nothing
> counts while you wait, so don't start working until it's approved.
>
> Nothing else changed: clocking in during your normal hours works exactly as
> before, with a half-hour grace for starting early.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** the staffing board (In office row + WFH tags, and the
click-a-shift menu now sets office/WFH too), Manage Time Clock (approval queue
at the top, office/WFH tag on each row), each person's manage page (a "where"
choice on every weekday, and on date exceptions), and the staff clock page.

**Why:** office staff mix office days and work-from-home days, and there was no
way to record which is which — or to catch someone clocking in on a day off or
hours outside their shift without checking by hand.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "I clocked in but the clock isn't running." Outside their hours, the punch is
  a request — nothing counts until you approve it (queue at the top of Manage
  Time Clock) and they press Clock In again. The clock starts at their press,
  never at your approval, so a late approval can't leave a ghost timer running.
- An approval is good for two hours; if they never start, it quietly lapses.
  If their request goes stale because their real window arrived and they
  clocked in normally, it clears itself from the queue.
- "Why wasn't so-and-so asked to request?" People with no weekly schedule set
  up are never gated — set their week first. There's also a 30-minute grace
  for clocking in early.
- To put someone on the clock immediately yourself, skip the request and use
  Punch in.
