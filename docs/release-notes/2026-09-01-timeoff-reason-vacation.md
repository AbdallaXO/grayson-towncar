# Time-off reasons no longer mention PTO

## Send this to the team

> Quick one — when you request time off, the reason list no longer says
> "PTO / vacation". It's just "Vacation" now, since we don't do PTO.
>
> Anything already booked under the old name now reads "Vacation" too.
>
> Nothing else changed: requesting time off, approvals, and the rest of the
> reason list (Sick, Personal, Appointment, Unpaid, Other) all work exactly
> as before.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** the reason dropdown on the My Schedule time-off request
form and the staffing board's "Book off" form.

**Why:** the company doesn't offer paid time off, so a "PTO" option taught
people something false. The driver side dropped PTO a while back; this brings
office staff in line.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "What do I pick for a vacation now?" — "Vacation". Same spot in the list.
- Old entries weren't deleted or changed — only the label on them.
