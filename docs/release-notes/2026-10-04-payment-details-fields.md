# Agents' payment details are checked when they're entered

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — how agents tell us to pay them is now proper fields instead of a free-text box, on sign-up, on their own profile, and in Edit profile on our side.
>
> - **PayPal:** their PayPal email
> - **Venmo:** the phone number on their Venmo (their @username is optional)
> - **Zelle:** email or phone
> - **Bank:** name, checking or savings, routing number, account number twice
> - **Agency:** nothing to enter, and they're linked to their agency when the name matches
>
> If something's wrong, like a bad routing number, it says so right away, and nothing is saved until it's fixed.
>
> Bank numbers show as ••••1234 on the profile. Click **Show** to see the full number.
>
> Cash App and Check are gone for new choices. Agents already on them keep them until they switch. Nobody's payments change today.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** the agent sign-up page, the agent's own profile, and Edit profile on the agent's page (Affiliate Management → click a name).

**Why:** The Oct 4 payout scan found 91 payees owed money we couldn't pay, almost all because the free-text box let agents type anything. Venmo @handles also can't go through PayPal's upload; a phone can.

**Expect to be asked:**
- *"An agent says their Venmo number is already filled in."* — For agents who only gave a @handle, we pre-fill the phone on their profile. They just confirm it.
- *"Can an agent switch their pay away from their agency?"* — No. If their agency collects their pay, their page says so, and only we can change it.
- *"What about the old bank details typed into notes?"* — They still show until someone re-enters them in the new bank fields. Re-entering them replaces the old note with ••••1234.
