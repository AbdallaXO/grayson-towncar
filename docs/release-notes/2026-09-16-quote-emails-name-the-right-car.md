# Quote emails name the right car

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — quote emails were naming the wrong car, and that's fixed as of today.
>
> If a guest wrote back confused — "I asked about a towncar but this says Mini Van" —
> they were right, and it wasn't their mistake or yours. The price was always correct.
> The car named above it sometimes wasn't.
>
> It only went wrong for guests who'd asked us for a quote more than once. The email
> would show the car from their *first* ask next to the price from their *newest* one.
> Same for the pickup spot, the date and whether it was one-way or round trip.
>
> Nothing about how you send a quote has changed — same screen, same button, same
> prices. You don't need to redo anything. New quotes going out from today are right.
>
> If a guest from the last few weeks is still holding a confused email, just quote them
> fresh and it'll come out correct.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** the quote email a guest gets after asking for a price.

**Why:** the email took its price from the newest quote but every other line — car,
route, date, one-way or round trip — from the guest's original enquiry. Those two match
for a first-time guest and drift apart for a returning one. 2,832 guests had already
been sent a quote naming the wrong car; it was running at roughly 680 a month and
climbing. The reported case was a $315 round-trip Sanford towncar sent under the
heading "Mini Van."

**Expect to be asked:**
- *"Were the prices wrong too?"* No. The price was always right for the car the guest
  actually asked about — it was the label beside it that was stale.
- *"Do I need to re-send old quotes?"* No. Only if a guest raises it, and then just
  send a fresh quote.
- *"Did the Book Now button send them to the wrong car?"* The button always used the
  correct car, which is why some guests booked the right vehicle despite the wrong
  label. Now the email and the button agree.
