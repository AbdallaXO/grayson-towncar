# The shop-window finder now picks a day the car has nothing on

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — when fleet looks for a day to take a car to the shop, the suggestion now picks a day that car has no trips on.
>
> 1. On the Fleet desk or the Outlook, pick the car and how many hours.
> 2. The best window is now the soonest day that car is free, often a day we haven't assigned yet. If every day has trips, it picks the day with the fewest.
> 3. Each day shows how many trips that car already has, like "8 trips on #004". The other options say how many trips they would move.
>
> Before, it could suggest a day the car was fully booked, as long as the rest of the fleet had room.
>
> Nothing else changed. It still skips weekends, and a day that would leave us short still comes last.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** Fleet → Desk (the window finder) and Fleet → Outlook.

**Why:** the finder only asked whether the fleet could spare one car. Pulling one Sprinter of five never leaves us short, so it recommended Tue 29 Sep for #004 even though #004 had 8 trips that day. It only mentioned them in the line underneath.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "Why is it suggesting Thursday and not tomorrow?" Tomorrow that car has trips; Thursday it has none yet. Book it now and nobody plans trips onto it.
- "Can I still pick the busy day?" Yes. Click any square on the grid. It tells you how many trips would move, and asks you to confirm before booking.
