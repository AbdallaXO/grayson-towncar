# Fleet can now book a car for part of the day, and you're asked before a trip lands on it

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — fleet can now book a car for a few hours, like a tyre slot or a detail, and you'll see it wherever you pick cars and drivers.
>
> 1. A booked car shows a small tag, like "Booked 10:00 AM–12:00 PM · Tire service", in the vehicle pool and the driver dropdown. On the schedule board it's a white block on the driver's row saying what it is and Soft or Hard. Hover for details.
> 2. If a trip you're assigning lands inside a booking, you're asked first. Press Continue Anyway to assign it, or Cancel to leave it.
> 3. A red lock tag means a hard booking, and the car can't run then. The trip won't go on it anywhere. Put it on another car, or call fleet.
>
> Nothing else changed. Cars without a booking work exactly as before, and whole days in the shop still work the same way.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** Fleet → The day. Fleet clicks an open stretch on a car's line, or "+ Book", and fills in the time, what it's for, where, and soft or hard. Clicking a booking opens it to change, harden or cancel. Dispatch sees the tags in both vehicle pools, on the schedule board and in the trip dropdowns. The question comes up on the legs dashboard, the reservation page, the legs list, conflict tasks, the planner and the board. The fleet desk lists any booking a trip has since landed on.

**Why:** Fleet could only take a car off the road for whole days, and had no way to say "I need #006 from ten to twelve". Dispatch found out about tyre appointments when the car wasn't where they expected.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "Can I still use an amber-booked car?" Yes. You're asked, and you choose. Fleet sees the clash on The day and moves the appointment if needed.
- "Who can make or lift a hard booking?" Only the fleet manager, or you. Anyone on staff can make, change or cancel a soft one.
- "Auto-assign, the Smart Builder, a swap or a publish skipped a trip." Its car was hard-booked then. They leave those trips unassigned and say which ones. Soft bookings are listed in the preview before you apply.
- "I moved a car to another driver and pressed Cancel." The first driver keeps the car. Nothing moves until you continue.
- "A flight moved a trip into a booking." Nobody is asked, because the flight decides that. The tag turns ⚠ on the board and the booking shows the conflict on The day.
- "Do drivers see it?" No. Nothing changed in the driver app.
