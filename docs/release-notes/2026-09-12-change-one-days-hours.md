# Change someone's hours for one day, right on the staffing board

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — when someone's hours move for a single day, you can now fix it on the
> staffing board instead of leaving the board wrong.
>
> 1. Open the staffing board on the week (or day) you're fixing.
> 2. Click the person's shift on that day.
> 3. Pick **Change the hours for this day**, type the new start and end, save.
>
> Same menu has **Mark this day off**. So a swap — Luis off Tuesday, working
> Saturday that week — is two clicks: mark Tuesday off, then click the empty
> Saturday cell and add the shift.
>
> Every one of these is for that date only. Nobody's normal week changes, and next
> week they're back to their usual hours on their usual days. Changed a day by
> mistake? Click the shift again and choose **Back to their usual hours**.
>
> Nothing else on the board moved.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** The staffing board — click any shift.

**Why:** The board could only add a shift to an empty day. If someone's hours moved
for one day, or they swapped a day that week, there was no way to say so short of
editing their standard week — which then had to be remembered and changed back.

**Expect to be asked:**
- *"Does this change their schedule going forward?"* No. It only touches that date.
  Their standard week is edited on their own schedule page, same as always.
- *"They already work that day — will this add a second shift?"* No. Changing the
  hours replaces that day's shift. A second shift is its own choice in the same menu.
- *"Can I do a few days at once?"* Yes — there's a *Through* date on both forms. Each
  day is still separate afterwards, so you can put one day back without losing the rest.
- *"What if they're booked off that day?"* It won't let you, and it says so. Remove
  the time off first.
