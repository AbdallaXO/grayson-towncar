# Choose exactly who goes in the PayPal & Venmo file

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — on the PayPal & Venmo batch page you can now choose exactly who to pay.
>
> 1. Pick **PayPal only** or **Venmo only** at the top, next to Everyone / Agents / Agencies.
> 2. Untick anyone you want to leave out. The total updates as you go.
> 3. **Download file**, send it in PayPal, then **Mark these paid**.
>
> After you download, the ticks lock so the file and what gets marked paid always match. If they don't match a file you downloaded, the app won't mark anyone paid and tells you why.
>
> Anyone you leave out stays owed and shows up next time. Nothing else changed.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** Affiliate Management → **PayPal & Venmo batch**.

**Why:** Paying from a PayPal balance means sometimes sending part of the list now and the rest later, or Venmo and PayPal on different days.

**Expect to be asked:**
- *"It said the people ticked don't match a file I downloaded."* — Tick exactly who was in the file you sent, or download a new file for the people ticked and send that one.
- *"I downloaded an Agents file and an Agencies file."* — Both work. Mark each one paid from its own view.
- *"I reloaded the page after downloading."* — Fine, as long as you tick the same people and their amounts haven't changed.
