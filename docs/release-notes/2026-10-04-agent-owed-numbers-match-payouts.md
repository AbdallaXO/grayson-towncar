# Agent "owed" numbers now match what we actually pay

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — the "owed" and "pending" amounts for travel agents now match what Pay Now and the PayPal batch pay.
>
> Before, those amounts only counted trips someone had clicked "completed". A trip past its date still got paid, but it didn't show in the owed number. That's why some agencies saw less than they were owed.
>
> Now a trip counts as owed the day after its last ride, whether or not anyone clicked "completed". The numbers update when a booking changes and recount every hour.
>
> Nothing about who gets paid or how much changed. Only the number shown is now correct.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** agency pages agencies see, agency detail, commission reports, the admin agent list.

**Why:** 49 agents' shown "owed" didn't match their payout (e.g. $237.80 shown vs $277.20 actually owed).

**Expect to be asked:**
- "An agent's owed number went up today?" Yes. Trips that already happened are now counted, matching what they'll be paid.
- "How fast does it update?" When a booking is saved, and at least once an hour.
