# Farm-out partners now get the Publix stop with the job

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — when we farm a job out to a partner company, the grocery stop now
> travels with it. Before, their copy of the job listed everything else — guest,
> flight, car seats, bags — but never mentioned Publix, so they'd send a driver
> who knew nothing about it and hadn't left time for it.
>
> Their job now carries a Store stop line: Publix @9930 Universal Blvd, 20
> minutes. It's on the copy button they paste into their own system, and it's on
> the job itself. It only shows on the leg going into town, never the ride back
> to the airport.
>
> Nothing changed for our own chauffeurs — they already saw the Publix stop, and
> their app looks exactly the same. Nothing changed about how you book or assign
> a grocery stop either.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** the partner (operator) portal — the job card and every Copy
button on it.

**Why:** the copy block was built field by field and the grocery stop was simply
never one of them. A partner re-keys our job into their own dispatch system, so
anything missing from that block does not exist as far as their driver is
concerned — and a 20-minute stop nobody planned for runs the whole chain late.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "Do I need to tell partners about the Publix stop now?" No — it's on their job
  and in the text they paste. Worth mentioning once so they look for it.
- "Does it show on the return leg?" No. Same rule the driver app uses: only the
  leg heading into town.
