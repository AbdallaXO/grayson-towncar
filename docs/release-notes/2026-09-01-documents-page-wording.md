# The My Documents page now says uploads are secure

## Send this to the team

> Quick one for the drivers — the My Documents page in the driver app now spells
> out that document photos travel over a secure, encrypted connection straight to
> the office, and are only used to keep your driver file current.
>
> Nothing about how it works changed: same three cards, same camera button, same
> automatic reading of your license details. Only the wording is new, so nobody
> has to wonder where their license photo ends up.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** My Documents, in the driver app.

**Why:** the old wording just said uploads "go straight to the office" — drivers
handing over a license photo deserve to be told it's transmitted securely and
what it's used for.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "Is it actually secure?" — yes: uploads go over the same encrypted (HTTPS)
  connection as everything else in the app, files are checked and stored under
  unguessable names, and only the office uses them, to keep driver files current.
