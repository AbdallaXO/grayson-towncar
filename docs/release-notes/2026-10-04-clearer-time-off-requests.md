# Asking for a few hours off is clearer in the driver app

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — asking for time off in the driver app is clearer now, especially when you only need a few hours.
>
> 1. Open Time Off from the menu and tap New request.
> 2. Pick Whole day(s) off or A few hours off.
> 3. For a few hours, fill in "I can't work from __ to __". Those are the hours you're OFF, not the hours you're working.
>
> Before you send it, a line underneath reads it back to you, like "You're off Fri, Oct 9 from 9 AM to 1:30 PM. You can still get trips before 9 AM and after 1:30 PM." If the times are backwards, it tells you.
>
> Your requests now say "Waiting for approval" until dispatch decides.
>
> Nothing else changed. Dispatch still approves every request, and anything you already sent is still there.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** the driver app, menu → Time Off (the My Requests list) and its New request
button.

**Why:** a part-day request only showed "From" and "To", so drivers couldn't tell if they were
entering the hours they work or the hours they're off. The screen now says it in words and
reads the request back before it's sent. Every status, error and "you already sent this"
message was reworded to match, dates read "Tue, Oct 6", and the request cards are readable in
dark mode (the dates used to vanish). When dispatch approves or denies a few-hours request,
the text the driver gets now reads "off 9:00 AM - 1:00 PM" instead of just the two times.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "Can I ask to work only 9 to 1?" Not from this screen. It takes one block of hours off
  per day. Have them tell dispatch, who can set a working window under Drivers → Edit
  Schedules.
- "Did my old requests disappear?" No. Everything already sent is still listed, with the new
  wording.
