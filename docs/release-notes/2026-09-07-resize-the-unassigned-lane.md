# Drag the Unassigned lane taller or shorter

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — the Unassigned strip at the top of the board is yours to size now.
>
> 1. Put your cursor on the gold line under the strip — there's a small handle in
>    the middle of it.
> 2. Drag it down to see more of the backlog at once, or up to get it out of the
>    way and give the drivers more room.
> 3. Double-click the handle to snap it back to the size it picks on its own.
>
> The size you set sticks — page to tomorrow, come back after lunch, it's still
> how you left it. On a day with nothing unassigned the strip stays small
> whatever you set, since there's nothing in it to look at.
>
> Nothing else moved. Same jobs, same lanes, drag and drop works exactly as it
> did, and drivers see nothing different.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** the gold rule under the Unassigned strip at the top of the
schedule board, on both the In-House and Affiliates sides.

**Why:** the lane sizes itself to fit the chips, capped so it can't swallow the
screen. That is right for an ordinary afternoon and wrong at 6am, when the whole
day is sitting in it and most of the backlog is behind a scroll.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "Does everyone get my size?" No — it's saved in that browser, for that person.
- "It went back to normal." Either they double-clicked the handle, or they're on
  a different computer or browser.
- "How big can it get?" Up to about four-fifths of the screen. Past that you'd be
  scrolling the page to find the bottom of it, which defeats the point.
