# The yellow permit photo now reads itself

## Send this to the team

> Hey team — when a driver photographs their yellow City of Orlando permit in
> the driver app, the system now reads the permit number, the FDL number, and
> the expiration date off the card for them.
>
> For drivers it works exactly like the license upload already does:
>
> 1. Open My Documents and take or upload the permit photo.
> 2. Check the details it read — fix anything that looks off.
> 3. Save.
>
> Before, a permit photo just sat there until the office typed the details in
> by hand. Now the driver confirms them on the spot.
>
> What did NOT change: the office still gets a text for every permit photo,
> so nothing slips through if a driver skips the confirm step or the photo is
> too blurry to read. The DOT medical card works the same as before — photo
> only, office fills it in.

---

## Behind the scenes

**Where it lives:** My Documents in the driver app — the Chauffeur Permit card.

**Why:** Permit photos were piling up untranscribed. The license already read
itself; the permit didn't, because the tool that reads licenses only
understands standard state IDs and returns nothing for a county permit card.
The permit now goes through a different reader that answers questions
against the photo ("what is the permit number?"), which handles the Orlando
card's printed labels.

**Expect to be asked:**
- "It said it couldn't read my permit." — The photo still saved, and they can
  type the details in right there. A flat, bright, straight-on shot reads
  best; PDFs and iPhone HEIC files upload fine but can't be read
  automatically.
- "The driver typed the wrong thing." — Same as the license: what the driver
  confirms is what saves, and the profile still shows the mismatch warning if
  the FDL number doesn't match the license on file. The office text still
  arrives on every upload, so spot-checking stays possible.
