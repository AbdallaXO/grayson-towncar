# Refund reminder for the team

@all
Hey team, quick but important note about requesting refunds.

I've seen a few trips get requested for refund the wrong way, and a couple slipped through. Here's the thing: when we approve a refund, we just trust that you requested it the right way. So it really matters that you pick the right option when you send it in. Just so we're all on the same page:

When you request a refund, there are 3 types. Please pick the right one.

**1. Price Adjustment**
The customer is keeping their trip. We are just giving some money back. For example: an overcharge, a little goodwill, a price fix, a vehicle downgrade, or dropping an after-hours fee. Nothing gets cancelled.

**2. Partial Cancellation**
They are dropping part of the trip but keeping the rest. For example, they cancel the return but keep the pickup. You pick exactly which leg to cancel.
Example: a $275 SUV round trip, and they cancel the return. You would request $135 to be refunded, select the return leg, and mark it as Partial Cancellation.

**3. Full Cancellation**
The whole reservation is cancelled. Every leg, the entire trip. Only use this when the customer is cancelling everything. This is still a Full Cancellation even when they cancel close to pickup and only get a partial refund (like 50%). It is still a Full Cancellation, just with a smaller refund amount.

The big one to watch: do not pick Full Cancellation unless the customer is cancelling the ENTIRE trip. That is the mistake we keep running into. Full Cancellation cancels every ride, drops all the drivers, and takes the reservation off the board so no driver gets assigned to it by mistake. If it is only about the price, or just one leg, use one of the other two.

Before you send it in, just ask yourself: is the whole trip really cancelled? If not, pick Price Adjustment or Partial.

If you ever slip up, don't stress, just tell me and I can fix it on my end. Let's just try to get the type right the first time, because once the money goes back to the card it can't be undone.

Thanks everyone.
