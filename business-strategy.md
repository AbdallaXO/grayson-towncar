# 🕵️ Grayson Towncar – Internal Business Model & Operations Summary

> Private strategic notes on how Grayson Towncar runs behind the scenes.

---

## 🚗 Overview

Grayson Towncar is a lean, service-based transportation company based in Orlando, Florida. We don’t own any vehicles — instead, we operate as a **dispatch and booking platform**, connecting travelers with vetted, professional drivers.

The goal is to provide **reliable, comfortable transportation** for airport travelers, Disney-area guests, cruise passengers, and other local riders — without the overhead of fleet ownership.

---

## 🧠 Business Model (Real Talk)

Grayson Towncar is structured as a **dispatcher-first business**. Our main role is:
- Attracting bookings (via website, SEO, referrals, social media)
- Collecting payment securely
- Dispatching trips to trusted, contracted drivers

We make money by charging customers **flat rates** per route, and paying drivers a **fixed portion** of that. We keep the margin in between.

### We Do NOT:
- Own cars
- Hire employees
- Handle physical vehicle maintenance

---

## 🛠️ How It Works (Behind the Curtain)

### Step-by-Step Flow:

1. **Customer finds our website** via SEO, social media, or referral
2. **Fills out a booking form** (pickup/dropoff, vehicle type, etc.)
3. **Pays via Stripe Checkout**
4. **Confirmation email is sent**
5. **We receive the reservation** in our admin panel
6. **We manually assign the ride** to a trusted independent driver
7. **Driver completes the trip**, we confirm status
8. **Stripe payout occurs**, we retain profit margin

---

## 🚘 Driver Model

- Drivers are **1099-style independent contractors**
- They own and maintain their own insured vehicles
- We build a pool of **go-to drivers** we trust and can dispatch to
- Pay is per trip (fixed or percentage-based)
- Communication is via text/phone (dispatch app later)

Benefits:
- No vehicle costs or maintenance
- Scale up/down as needed
- Keep overhead extremely low

## 👥 Target Clients

We serve a few key customer types:

### 🎢 1. Disney & Universal Travelers
- Families flying into MCO
- Vacationers going to resorts
- Often need car seats, larger vehicles

### 🚢 2. Cruise Passengers (Port Canaveral)
- Often need round trips (airport ➝ port ➝ resort)
- Great opportunity for bundled rides

### 💼 3. Business Travelers
- Need professional, quiet, clean rides
- Will pay more for reliability

### 🏠 4. Locals
- Occasional airport trips, weddings, events

---

## 🤝 Partners We Need

We're not just relying on Google traffic — we’re building relationships:

- **Travel Agents**: Offer them commission to book us for their clients
- **Hotel Front Desks & Concierges**: We give them a simple form or booking link
- **Airbnb & Vacation Rental Hosts**: Leave them cards or digital links for guests
- **Cruise Coordinators**: Build bundle deals for cruise customers
- **Wedding/Event Planners**: Upscale group rides

We want to build **trust and ease** with these partners — make it *easy* for them to send people our way and get paid.

---

## 💳 Pricing Strategy

- Fixed rates by route + vehicle (e.g., SUV from MCO ➝ Disney = $X)
- Optional add-ons: car seats, extra stops
- Flat-rate round-trip options
- Price high enough for profit, but competitive with UberXL & other shuttle companies
