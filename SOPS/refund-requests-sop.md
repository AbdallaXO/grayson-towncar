# SOP-001: Requesting Refunds

**Applies to:** Anyone who requests a refund (dispatchers and office staff)
**Approved by:** Management (management reviews and approves every refund)
**Last updated:** July 27, 2026

---

## Purpose

To make sure every refund is requested the right way. When we approve a refund, we trust that you picked the correct type. Picking the wrong type (usually a Full Cancellation by mistake) cancels rides that should not be cancelled, drops the drivers, and takes the trip off the board. This SOP explains how to get it right.

---

## The 3 refund types

Pick the one that matches what the customer actually wants.

### 1. Price Adjustment
The customer is **keeping their trip**. We are just giving some money back. Nothing gets cancelled.

Use it for:
- An overcharge
- A little goodwill credit
- A price fix
- A vehicle downgrade
- Dropping an after-hours fee

### 2. Partial Cancellation
The customer is **dropping part of the trip but keeping the rest**. You pick exactly which leg to cancel. The rest of the trip stays live.

Example: A $275 SUV round trip, and the customer cancels the return. You would request **$135** to be refunded, select the **return leg**, and mark it as **Partial Cancellation**.

### 3. Full Cancellation
The **whole reservation is cancelled**. Every leg, the entire trip. Only use this when the customer is cancelling everything.

This is still a Full Cancellation even when the customer cancels close to pickup and only gets a partial refund (for example 50%). It is still a Full Cancellation, just with a smaller refund amount.

---

## The #1 rule

**Do not pick Full Cancellation unless the customer is cancelling the ENTIRE trip.**

This is the mistake we keep running into. Full Cancellation cancels every ride, drops all the drivers, and takes the reservation off the board so it never gets a driver assigned by mistake. If it is only about the price, or just one leg, use Price Adjustment or Partial Cancellation.

Before you send a Full Cancellation, ask yourself: **is the whole trip really cancelled?** If not, pick one of the other two.

---

## How to request a refund (steps)

1. Open the reservation.
2. Click **Request Refund**.
3. Choose the **refund type** (Price Adjustment, Partial Cancellation, or Full Cancellation).
4. If it is a **Partial Cancellation**, tick the exact leg(s) being cancelled.
5. Check the **refund amount**. The system suggests an amount based on our policy. You can change it if needed, but add a reason.
6. Add a short **reason** for the refund.
7. Submit. The request goes to management to review and approve.

---

## Refund amount policy

The system suggests the amount for you based on how far before pickup the customer cancels:

| Time before pickup | Refund |
|--------------------|--------|
| 48 hours or more | 100% (full refund) |
| 24 to 48 hours | 50% |
| Less than 24 hours | 0% (non-refundable) |

You can still adjust the amount when there is a good reason, but leave a note so management understands why.

---

## If you make a mistake

Do not stress. Tell management right away and it can be fixed on our end. Just try to get the type right the first time, because once the money goes back to the customer's card it cannot be undone.

---

## Quick reference

| Situation | Refund type |
|-----------|-------------|
| Keeping the trip, just money back | Price Adjustment |
| Cancelling some legs, keeping others | Partial Cancellation |
| Cancelling the whole reservation | Full Cancellation |
