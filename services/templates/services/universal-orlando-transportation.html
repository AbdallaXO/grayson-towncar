How much is a private car from MCO to Universal Orlando?

A private car from Orlando International Airport to Universal Orlando hotels costs $105 one-way or $195 round-trip in an Executive Sedan seating up to 4. A Family Minivan seating up to 5 is $120 one-way / $230 round-trip. Every Grayson Towncar rate is all-inclusive: tolls, taxes, real-time flight tracking, baggage-claim meet & greet, and free Graco car seats are all in the price. There is no surge pricing and no mandatory gratuity add-on — the rate you book is the rate you pay, per vehicle, never per person. For every vehicle and route, see the full rate table.

That short drive should be the easiest part of your trip — and with a private car, it is. Your driver tracks your flight in real time and texts you when you land. If the flight runs early or late, your pickup time adjusts to the actual arrival at no charge. Your chauffeur meets you at baggage claim with a name sign, helps with your luggage, and drives straight to your Universal hotel. No bus stops, no rideshare queue, no app refreshing in a crowded terminal.

Traveling with little ones? Free Graco car seats — infant, toddler, and booster — are professionally installed on every booking, so you can leave your own seats at home. And every airport pickup includes a free 20-minute grocery stop at Publix on the way to your hotel.
