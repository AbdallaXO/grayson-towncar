What Is Universal's Epic Universe?

Universal's newest and most ambitious theme park — five immersive worlds connected by a celestial hub, located on a brand-new campus south of the original Universal Orlando Resort.

Celestial Park

The central hub connecting all five worlds, with dining, shopping, and its own attractions.

Epic Universe is on a separate campus from Universal Studios Florida and Islands of Adventure. Getting there requires its own transportation plan, especially if you're coming straight from the airport.

Why a Private Car Service Makes Sense for Epic Universe

Epic Universe sits on its own campus — separate from the main Universal resort. There's no monorail connecting it, no free resort bus from the airport, and no direct public transit. If you're flying into MCO, a private car service is the most direct way to get there without dealing with shuttle transfers, rideshare surge pricing, or hauling luggage through multiple stops.

Your driver tracks your flight in real time, texts you when you land, meets you at baggage claim, loads your luggage, and drives you straight to the park or your nearby hotel. Door to door, about 25 minutes.

The Problem

Uber Surge and Shuttle Delays at a Brand-New Park

New parks attract massive crowds. Here's what that means for your ride from the airport.

Uber & Lyft Surge Pricing at Peak Times

  • Park opening mornings and closing times trigger heavy surge — fares can double or triple
  • Special events, holidays, and weekends at a popular new park mean unpredictable pricing
  • Wait times at MCO can hit 15-30 minutes during peak arrival windows
  • No one meets you at baggage claim — you're on your own finding the rideshare pickup zone
  • If you need car seats for kids, Uber's Car Seat Mode has limited availability in Orlando

Shared Shuttles Aren't Built for This

  • Mears Connect makes multiple hotel stops — what should be 25 minutes becomes 45-90
  • Epic Universe's separate campus means shuttles may route through the main Universal resort first
  • Per-person pricing adds up for families and groups
  • You're on their schedule, not yours — limited departure times and long waits for the bus to fill
  • No room for flexibility if your flight is delayed or you want to stop for groceries
