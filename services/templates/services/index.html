Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Orlando hotel transportation services

Our driver will meet you at the baggage claim area with a sign displaying your name. We track your flight, so don't worry about delays. The driver will help with your luggage and escort you to your private vehicle for direct transportation to your Orlando hotel.

Yes, we provide direct transportation between any Orlando hotels, including transfers from Disney hotels to Universal hotels, International Drive accommodations, or any other Orlando area hotels. This is a private service with no additional stops.

We offer a complimentary 20-minute grocery stop at Publix on the way to your hotel if requested. This service is included with your airport pickup at no additional charge and allows you to stock your hotel room with essentials before check-in.

Yes, we provide transportation to all Orlando area hotels, including those in Lake Buena Vista, Kissimmee, Celebration, ChampionsGate, and other surrounding areas. We also service vacation home rentals and Airbnb properties throughout the greater Orlando region.
