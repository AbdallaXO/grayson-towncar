About Port Canaveral

Located just 45 miles east of Orlando, Port Canaveral is one of Florida's busiest cruise ports and a gateway to the Caribbean. As the second-busiest cruise port in the world, Port Canaveral welcomes millions of passengers each year aboard Disney Cruise Line, Royal Caribbean, Carnival, Norwegian, and MSC cruise ships.

Our transportation service provides seamless transfers between Orlando International Airport (MCO), Disney World resorts, and all terminals at Port Canaveral, ensuring you arrive relaxed and on time for your cruise departure.
