Know Your Terminal

We Pick Up at Every MCO Terminal

Orlando International has three terminals across two buildings. Your flight number tells us exactly where you land — your driver is at the right baggage claim either way.

Terminals A & B — Main Terminal

  • Two sides of MCO's main terminal building, serving most domestic carriers.
  • Your driver meets you at your baggage claim carousel with a name sign.
  • From bags to vehicle in minutes — no shuttle bus, no parking-garage hunt.

Terminal C — The New Terminal

  • MCO's newest terminal sits in its own building with its own roadways and baggage claim.
  • Serves many international flights and newer carriers — easy to end up here without realizing it.
  • We confirm your terminal from your flight number, so there is never a wrong-building mix-up.

Landing at the new terminal? Read our dedicated MCO Terminal C transportation guide for exactly where to meet your driver and how Terminal C pickups work.
