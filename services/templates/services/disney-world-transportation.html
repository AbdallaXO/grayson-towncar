The Service

Private Transfers from Orlando Airport to Disney World

Disney no longer runs a free airport shuttle — which means every family flying into MCO has to plan its own ride to the resort. Here is the simplest, most comfortable way to do it.

How much does a private car from MCO to Disney World cost?

A private car from Orlando International Airport (MCO) to any Walt Disney World resort starts at $105 one-way for an Executive Sedan (up to 4 passengers) or $195 for a round trip. A Family Minivan (up to 5 passengers) runs $120 one-way / $230 round-trip. All fares are all-inclusive — no surge pricing, no gratuity add-on, no hidden fees. Tolls, taxes, free car seats, baggage claim meet & greet, and a 20-minute Publix grocery stop are included.

Grayson Towncar is a family-owned Orlando transportation company, founded in 2022 and rated 4.8/5 across 1,500+ Google reviews. We run airport-to-Disney transfers all day, every day — and the trip is built around arriving families. Your driver tracks your flight in real time, texts you when you land, and meets you at baggage claim holding a sign with your name on it. No app refreshing at the curb, no hunting for a shuttle stop with tired kids and four suitcases.

If your flight is delayed, your pickup time adjusts to your actual arrival at no charge. Traveling with little ones? Free Graco car seats — infant, toddler, or booster — are professionally installed before your driver leaves for the airport. And every airport pickup includes a free 20-minute grocery stop at Publix, so you can walk into your resort room with breakfast, snacks, and drinks already in hand. Need a car seat for every leg of your trip? See our free car seat program.

Pricing is per vehicle, never per person — so a family of five pays one flat fare, not five tickets. The price you book is the price you pay, day or night, holiday or not. We answer the phone 24/7 at (407) 212-7190.

Direct Drive vs. Shared Shuttle

A private car covers the roughly 24 miles from MCO to the Disney World resort area in 25-35 minutes, driving straight to your resort's front entrance. During peak holiday weeks, plan on 40-50 minutes.

The Mears Connect shared shuttle runs the same route in 60-90 minutes, because the bus stops at multiple resorts before yours — and you find the bus stop yourself with your luggage. If you are weighing the two options, read our full Mears Connect alternative comparison or our step-by-step MCO to Disney World route guide.
