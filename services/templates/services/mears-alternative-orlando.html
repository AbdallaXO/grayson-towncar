The Situation

Life After Disney's Magical Express

When Disney's Magical Express ended in January 2022, Mears Connect became the default MCO shuttle to the Disney World resorts. To be fair: it works. Flights are tracked, and at $17.60 per adult one-way it is the cheapest seat to Disney for a solo traveler.

But it is a shared shuttle: per-person pricing, a bus loading area you find yourself, and multiple hotel stops before yours — typically 60–90 minutes door to door. For a family with luggage and tired kids, that math and that hour both matter.

This page lays out the alternatives — rideshare, private car services like ours, and other towncar operators — with honest numbers. Already set on a private transfer? See our MCO to Disney World route guide.

Do the Math

When a Private Car Beats Mears on Price

The break-even is simple: Mears charges per seat, a private car charges per vehicle.

Is a private car cheaper than Mears Connect for a family of 4?

For a family of 4 adults, Mears Connect Standard charges $17.60 per adult — approximately $70.40 one-way to Disney World ($140.80 round-trip). Children ages 3–9 add $14.30 each. A Grayson Towncar private sedan costs $105 one-way for the entire vehicle — not per person. For 3 adults traveling together, Mears ($52.80) is cheaper. For 4 or more passengers, private car at $105 per vehicle becomes cost-competitive — and includes free car seats, direct delivery to your resort, and a 20-minute grocery stop that Mears does not offer.

Every Grayson rate is all-inclusive — no surge, no mandatory gratuity — and never climbs with extra seats. See the full rate table or our Disney World transportation page.

The Full Picture

The Other Alternatives, Honestly

We are one of several good options — here is the whole field, fairly stated.

What are the best alternatives to Mears in Orlando?

The main alternatives to Mears Connect for MCO airport transportation are: (1) Private car services such as Grayson Towncar (from $105 per vehicle, all-inclusive) and Ace Luxury Transportation (from $140 + 20% gratuity); (2) Rideshare apps Uber and Lyft (typically $30–$60 one-way, subject to surge pricing); (3) Tiffany Towncar (from $100 per vehicle, gratuity not included). For families with car seats, large groups, or travelers wanting a direct-to-resort experience with no shared stops, private car services offer the best value.

When Mears is the right call: solo travelers and couples on a budget. Two adults pay $35.20 one-way — no private service matches that.

When rideshare is the right call: light packers, no kids, off-peak arrival — if you can accept surge pricing, curb pickup, and no car seats.

Among private operators, compare what is included. Ace's $140 base is roughly $168 after its mandatory 20% gratuity; Tiffany's $100 excludes gratuity. Grayson's $105 includes tolls, taxes, car seats, and meet & greet, with no required tip — see the full rate table.
