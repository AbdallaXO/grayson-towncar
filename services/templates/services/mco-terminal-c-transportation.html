The New Terminal

What Is MCO Terminal C?

Orlando International Airport opened Terminal C in 2022 — a brand-new, state-of-the-art facility south of the original terminals. If your airline operates from Terminal C, the pickup process is different from what you might be used to.

New Terminal

Terminal C

The newest addition to MCO, with modern design and its own ground transportation level.

  • JetBlue (most Orlando flights)
  • Select international carriers
  • Connected via Automated People Mover
  • Baggage claim on Level 6
  • Ground transportation on Level 1

Original Terminals

Terminals A & B

The main terminal complex that most domestic airlines use, connected to the North and South terminals.

  • Delta, American, United, Southwest
  • Frontier, Spirit, Sun Country
  • Most domestic airlines
  • Baggage claim on Level 2
  • Ground transportation on Level 1

Not sure which terminal you're arriving at? It doesn't matter for your booking. We track your flight and know exactly which terminal and gate you're arriving at. Your driver will be at the right baggage claim regardless — Terminal C, Terminal A, or Terminal B.

Step by Step

How Your Terminal C Pickup Works

Terminal C has its own ground transportation area. Here's exactly what happens when you land.

1
You Land — We Already Know

We track your flight in real time. If you're early, delayed, or diverted, we know before you do. Your driver adjusts automatically.

2
Collect Your Bags at Terminal C Level 6

Head to the Terminal C baggage claim on Level 6. Your bags will arrive on the carousel just like any other terminal.

3
Meet Your Driver on Level 6

Your driver is waiting near the escalators and elevators on Level 6 with a greeting sign. They'll walk you to your baggage carousel and help you collect your luggage.

4
Walk to Your Vehicle

Your driver helps with luggage and walks you to your private vehicle on Level 1. If you have kids, car seats are already installed and ready.

5
Direct to Your Destination

Straight to Disney, Universal, your hotel, or anywhere in the Orlando area. Want to stop at Publix for groceries on the way? It's included — just ask.

The Problem

Rideshare Pickup at Terminal C Is Confusing

Terminal C is new, the layout is unfamiliar, and the rideshare pickup process isn't what most travelers expect.

Uber & Lyft at Terminal C

  • You have to find the designated rideshare pickup zone on Level 1 — it's not where you'd expect
  • Request your ride from the app, then wait at the curb for a driver to show up
  • Peak arrival times mean 10-30 minute waits, standing outside with luggage and kids
  • Surge pricing hits hardest during afternoon and evening arrival waves
  • No one inside the terminal to help — you're on your own navigating the new layout

Private Car Service at Terminal C

  • Your driver greets you on Level 6 with a sign, walks you to baggage claim, and helps with luggage
  • You get a text with their name, photo, and phone number before landing
  • Zero wait time — they're already there when you walk out
  • Fixed pricing, no surge regardless of time or demand
  • Help with luggage, car seats pre-installed, Publix stop available
