The Other Orlando Airport

Landing at Sanford? You're 45-55 Miles from the Parks

Orlando Sanford International Airport (SFB) is Allegiant Air's Orlando base — a smaller, calmer alternative to MCO that handles millions of budget travelers every year. There's a catch most first-time visitors discover only after they land: SFB sits northeast of Orlando, about 45-55 miles from the tourist corridor. The "Orlando" on your boarding pass is actually a 50-to-65-minute drive from Disney World.

Unlike MCO, Sanford arrivals can't count on a shared Disney shuttle — most travelers use private transfers, rideshare, or rental cars. For families with luggage and car seats — or anyone who just spent a flight in an Allegiant seat — a private car already waiting at baggage claim is the practical choice. You book once, the price is locked, and the driver handles the rest.
