# Grayson Towncar – Business & Website Overview

## 🚗 Business Overview

**Grayson Towncar** is a transportation service based in **Orlando, Florida**, offering reliable, professional airport shuttle and point-to-point transportation. While the service is tailored for **Disney-area visitors**, it's available to **all travelers** including families, tourists, and locals.

---

## 🛎️ Core Services

- Airport Shuttle Service (to/from MCO)
- Disney, Universal Studios, and resort transportation
- Cruise Port Service (e.g., Port Canaveral)
- One-way and Round-trip rides
- Car seats available upon request
- Manual dispatching and driver tracking (initial phase)

---

## 🚘 Fleet Options

- Town Car  
- SUV  
- Mini-Van  
- Van
