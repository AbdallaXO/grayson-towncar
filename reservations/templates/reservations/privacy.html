1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you request a quote, make a reservation, or otherwise contact us through our Services. The personal information we collect may include:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address
  • Reservation Details: Pickup and drop-off locations, travel dates and times, flight information, number of passengers, luggage details, and vehicle preferences
  • Payment Information: Credit card or debit card details, billing address (processed securely through third-party payment processors)
  • Communication Data: Messages, emails, and SMS text messages exchanged between you and Grayson Towncar
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our website, such as pages visited

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To process and fulfill your transportation reservations
  • To send you booking confirmations, trip reminders, driver details, and pickup updates via email and/or SMS
  • To respond to your quote requests and inquiries
  • To communicate with you about your reservations and our services
  • To process payments and prevent fraud
  • To improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. HOW WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION

We do not sell, rent, trade, lease, or otherwise share your personal information with any third parties for any purpose. Your information is used exclusively by Grayson Towncar to provide our transportation services to you. We do not share your data with advertisers, marketing companies, data brokers, or any other outside parties.

The only exception is if we are required to disclose your information by law, such as in response to a valid court order or government request.

4. SMS TEXT MESSAGING AND PHONE DATA

Consent to Receive SMS Messages

By providing your mobile phone number and submitting a quote request or making a reservation through our Services, you expressly consent to receive recurring automated SMS text messages from Grayson Towncar at the phone number you provide. These messages may include, but are not limited to:

  • Quote Requests: Follow-up messages about your transportation needs, rate information, and confirmation requests
  • Reservation Notifications: Booking confirmations, trip reminders, driver information, pickup updates, and other reservation-related notifications
  • Service Updates: Important information about changes to your scheduled transportation

How We Use Your Phone Number and SMS Data

Your phone number is collected solely for the purpose of providing transportation-related communications. We use your phone number to:

  • Send reservation confirmations and updates
  • Provide real-time pickup and driver information
  • Respond to quote requests and inquiries
  • Send trip reminders and schedule changes

No Sharing of SMS Data with Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, loan, trade, lease, or otherwise transfer for profit any phone numbers or personal information collected through our SMS messaging program to any third party. Your phone number and any data collected via SMS will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for any purpose, including marketing or promotional purposes. All SMS data, including phone numbers and message content, is kept strictly within Grayson Towncar and is used solely to communicate with you about your transportation services.

SMS Consent Is Not a Condition of Purchase

You are not required to consent to receive SMS messages as a condition of purchasing any goods or services from Grayson Towncar. However, if you do not consent, you may not receive important updates about your quote requests or reservations via text message.

Opting Out of SMS Messages

If at any time you wish to stop receiving SMS messages from us, you may opt out by replying "STOP" to any text message you receive from us. After opting out, you will receive a final confirmation message, and no further SMS messages will be sent to you unless you re-subscribe. You may reply "HELP" to any message for assistance or contact information. You may also contact us at contact@graysontowncar.com or call 407-212-7190 to request removal from our SMS messaging list.

Message Frequency and Rates

Message frequency varies based on your interactions with our Services (e.g., quote requests, reservations). Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan. Grayson Towncar is not responsible for any charges incurred from your carrier.
