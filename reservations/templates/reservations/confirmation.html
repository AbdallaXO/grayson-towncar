Airport Pickup Instructions

Our drivers monitor your flight's arrival time to stay updated on delays or early landings. Once your plane lands, your chauffeur will send you a text message to confirm their presence and guide you. They will then meet you inside the baggage claim area holding a sign with your name.

Gratuity

Gratuity is not included in the total price. We kindly recommend a 20% tip for your professional driver, which can be given in cash or added upon request.

Cancellation Policy

We offer a full refund for cancellations made 48 hours or more before your pickup time. For round-trip bookings canceled within 24 hours:

  • Return trip will be refunded in full
  • Arrival trip will receive a 50% credit
