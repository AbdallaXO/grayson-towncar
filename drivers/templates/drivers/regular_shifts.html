Switching moves every driver with a regular shift from today's hours to his regular shift: auto-assign plans his day around it, held to 12 hours from leaving base to getting back; the schedule board shows his hours and checks moves against them; and Day Setup follows his regular days on and off. It can be switched over once every driver has a regular shift, and back at any time. Nothing is sent to drivers, and the driver app stays the same.
