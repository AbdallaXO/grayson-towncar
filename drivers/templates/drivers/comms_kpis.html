What these count. The chauffeur taps a button in the driver app and our text opens in his own Messages app, so the guest can reply to the man driving them. A phone never reports back, so each figure is a message sent from the app — not a delivery receipt, and he may have edited it before sending. A tap made with no signal isn’t recorded, so these under-count rather than over-count.
Denominator. Live by trip stage. A trip enters the On the way count the moment the chauffeur marks himself on the way, the On location count when he arrives, and the Review count once the guest is aboard — nothing waits for the trip to be completed. A text sent before the status was touched still counts, one for one. Cancellations are excluded, and nothing before {{ tracking_start|date:"M j, Y" }} is counted; tracking did not exist. Re-sends to the same guest count once. Affiliates are excluded — they see the same buttons but are not held to the in-house standard — and operators never reach a card with a guest number.
