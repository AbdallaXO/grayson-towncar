What these count. The chauffeur taps a button in the driver app and our text opens in his own Messages app, so the guest can reply to the man driving them. A phone never reports back, so each figure is a message sent from the app — not a delivery receipt, and he may have edited it before sending. A tap made with no signal isn’t recorded, so these under-count rather than over-count.
Denominator. Completed trips only, cancellations excluded, through today — a trip counts the moment it’s marked completed, so today’s rate can still move as more trips finish. Nothing before {{ tracking_start|date:"M j, Y" }} is counted; tracking did not exist. Re-sends to the same guest count once. Affiliates and operators are excluded — their job cards carry no guest phone number, so they have no chance to take.
