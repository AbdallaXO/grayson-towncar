Partner terms
  1. 01
    Your account

    You are responsible for accurate account and booking information, and for having the authority to represent any agency you register.

  2. 02
    How commission works

    Client bookings earn the commission rate assigned by Grayson on the eligible base fare. Personal trips, gratuity and excluded charges do not earn commission. Commission is released once the trip is eligible, your identity is verified and a payee is confirmed.
