Getting Started

Welcome to Your Agency Dashboard

As an agency head, you have access to powerful tools to manage your agency and agents. Here's what you can do:

  • View and manage all your agents
  • Track commissions and payments
  • Process commission payouts
  • Monitor agency performance

Managing Agents

Agent Management Guide

Adding New Agents
  1. Go to the "View All Agents" page
  2. Click "Add New Agent"
  3. Fill in the agent's information
  4. Set their commission rate and payment preferences
Monitoring Agent Performance
  • View individual agent dashboards
  • Track their commission earnings
  • Monitor their reservation activity
  • Review their payment history

Commission Management

Understanding Commissions

Commission Types
  • Pending: Commissions from confirmed but not completed reservations
  • Unpaid: Commissions from completed reservations awaiting payment
  • Paid: Commissions that have been paid out
Processing Payments
  1. Review unpaid commissions in the dashboard
  2. Verify reservation completion
  3. Process payments through the commission history page
  4. Add notes for payment tracking
