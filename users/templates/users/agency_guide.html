Commission System Overview

How Agency Commission Payouts Work

Our commission system is designed to simplify your agency operations. All commission payments (10% of reservation value) are consolidated and paid directly to your agency account.

  • Agents track their individual 10% commission earnings
  • All actual payments flow to your agency account
  • You maintain full control over agent compensation splits
  • Detailed breakdowns help you manage internal payouts
Commission Status Categories
Pending

Commissions from confirmed reservations that haven't been completed yet. Payments processed only after service completion.

Awaiting Payment

Commissions from completed reservations ready for payment processing. Included in your next payout cycle.

Paid

Commissions processed and paid to your agency account. View detailed payout history and agent contributions.
