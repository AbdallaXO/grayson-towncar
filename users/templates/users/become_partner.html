II. The partner toolkit

Eight details we never compromise on — so you don’t have to.

Every detail below is standard on every partner booking. Not a tier you climb to. Not an upgrade. Not an add-on. The baseline.

Private vehicles

Black SUVs and sedans. Lincoln Navigators and Suburbans. Your client and their party only — no shared shuttles, no detour stops.

Free car seats

Infant, convertible, and booster seats. Installed and tested before the family clears baggage claim. No fee, on every family booking — because nobody should hand a crying toddler back to mom while a chauffeur fumbles with the latches.

Inside baggage claim

Suited chauffeur waiting inside the terminal with a personalized name sign. Your client never wonders where to go.

Flight tracking, 24h out

Flight delayed three hours? We already know. Your client doesn’t. We adjust the pickup quietly, before they ever look at their phone — and you never get the text from the curb.

Flat-rate pricing

No surge pricing. No delay fees. The quote your client sees at booking is what we charge — even if the flight lands four hours late.

Book however you like

Send clients to your branded referral link, reserve on their behalf in the partner dashboard, or pick up the phone. Commission attributes either way.

10% commission, paid clean

Tracked in your dashboard. Reconciled on a regular cycle. Paid without you ever chasing a statement.

Confirmations, on brand

Confirmation emails, arrival updates, and post-trip receipts go to your client. You’re copied on every one, so you’re never the last to know.
