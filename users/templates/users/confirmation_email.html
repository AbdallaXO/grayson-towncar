Airport Pickup

Our drivers monitor your flight in real time. Once you land, please take your phone off airplane mode right away. Cancellation Policy

Full refund for cancellations 48+ hours before pickup. For round-trip bookings canceled within 24 hours: return leg refunded in full; arrival leg receives a 50% credit.

This stop must be added to your reservation in advance — we are unable to accommodate same-day requests. We allow up to 20 minutes at Publix (9930 Universal Blvd). Guests may pre-order via Instacart to save time. In the event of a flight delay, the grocery stop may not be possible. If the stop exceeds 20 minutes, a $30 charge will apply. After that, you will be billed $1 per minute.

Departure Grace Period

Please have your full party and all luggage ready at the scheduled pickup time. Your driver will wait up to 10 minutes. If no one is present by then, the trip will be marked as a no-show and your fare will not be refunded.

If extended wait time is needed, a $30 fee applies for up to 15 additional minutes, subject to driver availability.

Cleaning Fee Policy

We take great care of our vehicles. In the event of a significant mess or soiling inside the car (including vomit or similar), a cleaning fee of $200 will be charged.

If a child seat is soiled, a flat $250 replacement fee applies. For safety reasons, a soiled car seat cannot be reused and must be replaced entirely.
